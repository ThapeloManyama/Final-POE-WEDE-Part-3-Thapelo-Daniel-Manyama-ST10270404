A Little Flour, A Lot of Heart

What began in 2018 as a tiny home oven and a stubborn sourdough starter has grown into Mokopane's most-loved artisan bakery. We still bake the same way — slowly, by hand, and with ingredients we're proud to name.

Our team of eight bakers arrives before sunrise to shape, score, and bake every loaf. We mill a portion of our own flour, source dairy from local farms, and roast our coffee beans just down the road. Nothing is rushed; everything is tasted.

Inside the Bakery

A peek behind the counter — from the crackling crusts of our sourdough to the buttery layers of our pastries and the colourful deli boards we build every morning.

Discover More · Interactive

Our signature sourdough is fermented for 36 hours using a 30‑year‑old starter. The result is a crackling crust, open crumb, and a gentle tang that pairs perfectly with our deli cheeses. Every loaf is hand‑shaped and baked in a stone hearth oven.

Start your day with buttery croissants, pain au chocolat, or our seasonal danishes. Everything is laminated by hand and baked fresh at 5am. Pair with a single‑origin espresso for the complete experience.

Our deli counter features local artisan cheeses, cured meats, and house‑made preserves. Create your own board or let our team guide you. Perfect for picnics, gatherings, or a simple evening treat.

We serve direct‑trade coffee roasted locally. Every cup is crafted with care — and our communal table is always open. Join us for a tasting, a book, or a friendly chat. It's where the neighbourhood meets.

Our Daily Breads

From classic country sourdough to seeded rye and olive fougasse — each loaf is fermented slowly and baked to order. We mill some of our own flour for maximum freshness.

  • Country Sourdough — 36h ferment, stone‑baked
  • Seeded Rye — caraway, sunflower, flax
  • Olive & Rosemary Fougasse — olive oil, sea salt
  • Baguette Tradition — crisp crust, open crumb

Hand‑Laminated Pastries

Our pastry chef folds layer upon layer of European butter into every creation. Baked throughout the morning so there's always something warm.

  • Butter Croissant — 27 layers, flaky perfection
  • Pain au Chocolat — Valrhona dark chocolate
  • Almond Danish — house‑made frangipane
  • Seasonal Fruit Galette — fresh, local fruit
