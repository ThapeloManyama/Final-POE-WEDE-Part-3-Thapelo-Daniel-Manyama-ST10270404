Our Story

From a small kitchen and a big dream to a destination built around craftsmanship, community, and unforgettable flavour.

Founded in 2018, Mokopane Artisan Bakery & Deli began as a humble kitchen built around a bold and meaningful dream: to revive the honest, time-honoured traditions of slow-fermented breads, handcrafted pastries, and wholesome deli fare that generations before us knew and cherished.

What started as a small idea soon grew into a passion for creating food that celebrates patience, craftsmanship, and authentic flavour. We wanted to bring back the simple pleasure of bread made with care — bread that is allowed the time to develop its character, aroma, texture, and distinctive taste.

Over the years, our vision has continued to grow, but our founding principles have remained unchanged. We believe that exceptional food begins with quality ingredients, skilled hands, traditional techniques, and a genuine respect for the craft.

Today, Mokopane Artisan Bakery & Deli continues to honour the traditions that inspired us while embracing creativity and modern tastes. From a small kitchen and a big dream to a destination built around craftsmanship, community, and unforgettable flavour — our story is still being baked, one loaf at a time.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver exceptional artisan breads, handcrafted pastries, and thoughtfully prepared deli products that consistently exceed our customers' expectations. We are committed to creating food that is not only delicious and memorable, but also prepared with integrity, passion, and an unwavering dedication to quality.

We strive to preserve the timeless traditions of artisan baking while embracing creativity, innovation, and contemporary culinary standards. From carefully selected ingredients to the final presentation, every product is crafted with precision, patience, and genuine care.

Our Vision

To become a trusted industry leader known for innovation, unwavering customer satisfaction, and sustainable growth while keeping the soul of artisan baking alive for generations to come.

We envision a future where exceptional food and genuine hospitality continue to bring people together, creating lasting memories and strengthening our community — one loaf, one pastry, one cup of coffee at a time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you visit. Can't find your answer? Chat to our team below.

Our first bake comes out at 07:00 sharp every morning — sourdough, croissants, and baguettes. A second batch of pastries and cakes usually comes out around 11:00. For the best selection, we recommend visiting before 10:00.
Absolutely. We offer a range of gluten-free and vegan-friendly options, including seeded gluten-free loaves and plant-based pastries. Please speak to our staff about specific allergens — our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, and nuts, so cross-contact is possible.
Yes! Custom cakes are one of our specialities. We recommend placing your order at least 5 days in advance for standard designs and 2 weeks for elaborate celebration cakes. You can chat directly to Lerato, our Pastry Chef, through the chat widget on this page.
We offer in-store collection for all orders, and local delivery within Mokopane for orders over R350. Delivery is usually arranged for the same day if ordered before 12:00. Contact us to confirm delivery slots and fees.
Use the chat button at the bottom-right of this page. You can choose to speak directly to Thandi (baking), James (deli), Lerato (pastries), or David (coffee & orders). They'll reply right there in the chat — or we'll connect you on WhatsApp if preferred.
You'll find us at 23 Church Street, Mokopane, Limpopo, 0600 — right in the heart of town. There is street parking nearby. See the interactive map in our Contact section for directions.
